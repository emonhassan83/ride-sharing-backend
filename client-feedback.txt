Split Ride Concept
Split Ride is a passenger transportation advance (pre)booking platform. Its sole purpose is to match riders and drivers under two models. These are:
Private Ride: a rider books for a ride where he/she does not want to share the ride with other people unfamiliar to him/her. The ride does not necessarily constitute that there will not be more than one (1) rider in a private labeled ride. ie a family of three (3) or a group of three (3) people sharing the ride as a Private ride option.
Payment is achieved by charging one riderÆs credit facilities (rider who ordered).
Split Ride: a rider books for a ride with the option to split the ride with another rider (or more) who has chosen the same type of ride in order to reduce their individual cost.
Payment is achieved by splitting the charge equally between the riders who have initiated the order.
Bear in mind that, at present, all rides within the Split Ride are online payments through the platform's infrastructure.
Invoicing
There are two types of Invoicing within the Split Ride ecosystem. There are:
To Rider: invoicing on behalf of the driver for the final price charged at the end of the ride with all legal details required from the driver.
To Driver: monthly invoices for the Technology Subscription Commission fees charged to the driver for accessing the platform.

Drivers are charged a Subscription Technology Commission fee on the total earnings generated by the Split Ride ecosystem.
Pricing
Please note:
The minimum price for using the Split Ride ecosystem as a Rider, regardless of pricing fares is set, for the time being at: Ç20
Prices should rounded up to the upper Ç5. ie if Ç16 then rounded to Ç20
Price Modification Constant rate for Private Rides is the % rate we add on top of the final price so as to achieve certain price effects for various purposes. It is not a surcharge but rather a deviation rate on top of the final pricing as this will be incorporated in the driverÆs earned gross fare. This rate may also be a negative rate depending on the use case.
Price Modification Constant rate for Split Rides is the % rate we add on top of the final price so as to achieve certain price effects for various purposes. It is not a surcharge but rather a deviation rate on top of the final pricing as this will be incorporated in the driverÆs earned gross fare.
Our model accounts for Upfront Pricing where the Calculated Price should be considered as a Locked and Final Price since each Ride should allow for One (1) Pickup and One (1) Drop Off (no multiple stops or drop offs in between to pick up other passengers of the same Ride order) other passengers so as to achieve the Split Ride concept without any unforeseen issues that multiple stops might cause.
No Luggage or additional Public Holiday fees will be implemented other than a rule for Split Rides request: one (1) luggage per passenger of a specific size. In the case of Public Holiday dates, we may decide to change the PMCrfPR or PMCrfSR for those specific dates.
